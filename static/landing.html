AGENT PIPELINE

Detection to runbook, without a human in the loop until it matters.

Every stage writes its rationale to the activity log, so the chain that produced a procedure is reconstructable after the fact.

📡
01 · MONITOR

Trend Detection

Scans 12 telemetry channels against nominal envelopes and rate-of-change history to catch drift before threshold breach.

🔬
02 · DIAGNOSTICS

Root Cause

Correlates the anomaly across subsystems and orbital context — eclipse, phase, thermal load — to separate cause from symptom.

🧭
03 · RECOVERY PLANNER

Ranked Options

Generates candidate procedures ranked by risk, recovery time, and consumable cost rather than a single fixed response.

🛰️
04 · DIGITAL TWIN

Simulate & Validate

Runs each option against mission constraints. Rejected procedures trigger a constrained fallback and revalidation.

🔐
05 · APPROVAL ROUTER

Severity Gating

LOW and MEDIUM execute autonomously. HIGH and CRITICAL hold for operator authorisation — the safety envelope is never self-widened.

📄
06 · RUNBOOK GENERATOR

Auditable Output

Emits a timestamped operator runbook: commands, expected telemetry response, abort criteria, and the reasoning behind the choice.

WHY IT SCALES

Rule-based automation breaks on novel faults.

Thresholds catch what someone already anticipated. Cross-subsystem interactions during an on-orbit event are exactly the cases nobody wrote a rule for.

⚡

Cross-subsystem reasoning

A thermal excursion that starts as a heater fault and ends as a battery derate is one event, not three alarms. Diagnostics traces the dependency instead of firing on each channel.

🧪

Validated before executed

No procedure reaches an operator without a digital-twin pass. Constraint violations force revision, not a warning label.

📋

Auditable by construction

Each agent logs its input, rationale, and output. The runbook cites the diagnosis it came from — reviewable long after the pass ends.

🔌

Runs fully offline

A scenario-aware mock reasoning layer keeps the whole pipeline demonstrable with no API key and no network — useful for air-gapped ground segments.

⏱️

Built for constellations

Cost per satellite is dominated by operator attention. Autonomous handling of routine severity bands is what makes fleet growth affordable.

🎛️

Injectable scenarios

Five failure modes — undervoltage, thermal runaway, attitude drift, memory exhaustion, link loss — injectable on demand to rehearse response.
